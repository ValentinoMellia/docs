Dual-Hold Saga Choreography (PRD & E-Commerce Compliant)

        

          Dual Hold protection: Market secures stock first, Bank secures coins second, Inventory delivers third, and Bank debits fourth:
        


        

          

            
PHASE 0 (MARKET)

            
0. Local Stock Hold

            
Atomic DB decrement. Rejects in 2ms if sold out (409 Conflict).

          

          

            
PHASE 1 (BANK)

            
1. Coin Balance Hold

            
Locks student coins on bank.holds.commands. Returns holdId (PENDING).

          

          

            
PHASE 2 (INVENTORY)

            
2. Asset Delivery

            
Transfers to inventory.items.commands. Confirmed as ITEM_PROVISIONED.

          

          

            
PHASE 3 (SETTLEMENT)

            
3. Final Debit & Stock Commit

            
Bank commits ledger deduction and Market commits StockHold (COMMITTED).

Spring Boot REST Endpoints & Contracts (English)

        
Official OpenAPI / Controller definitions for Market with Stock Hold support:


        

          
POST /api/v1/market/orders

          
Checkout initialization endpoint. Creates StockHold in 2ms. Returns 202 Accepted with orderId and stockHoldId, or 409 Conflict if out of stock.

        


        

          
GET /api/v1/market/templates

          
Retrieves available base templates (SHIELD, BOOST_XP, BOOST_COINS, LIFE).

        


        

          
POST /api/v1/market/courses/{courseId}/catalog/items

          
Allows professors to instantiate a template with custom coinPrice, stock limit, charges or mode.

        


        

          
GET /api/v1/market/courses/{courseId}/catalog

          
Student storefront view displaying active offerings, prices, and remaining stock units for the cohort.
